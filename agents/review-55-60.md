## ep 55: Cat eyes glow (30.5 s), 6/10

**P0**
- **7.5-15.0 s, abstract stretch at the 10 s wall.** At 7.5 s we see a red ring with a grey lens on a dark plum background. Nothing says "eye", the caption is invisible (still fading in), and there is no top label. From 11.5 to 15 s a green strip and a stack of red bars with drifting dots ("light in", "1st try", "2nd chance") stay on screen for 3.5 s with almost no change. A viewer cannot say what they are looking at at the exact moment people leave. **Fix:** keep the cat's real eye on screen and push in, then cut the eye open in place. Show one big photon (a glowing ball about 80 px) passing the retina, hitting a mirror that shines like chrome (tapetum, with a specular highlight), and coming back. Delete the bar scene, or make it a 1 s insert at most. Light the diagram so it fills at least 60 % of the frame.

**P1**
- 1.0-2.0 s: the glow switches off (the flashlight is drawn dark) for about 1 s. On a first watch this looks like a glitch. **Fix:** start with the light off at 0 s, then turn it on at about 0.3 s so the flicker reads as cause and effect. Or keep the glow on all the time.
- 6.0-7.0 s: the "bounce back" rays are thin yellow and green hairlines over the cat's face, unreadable on a phone. **Fix:** draw one thick beam (12 px or more) going in and a coloured beam coming out, plus a "bounce" SFX.
- 12.0 s: the "mirror" label is dark text on a dark background and almost invisible. **Fix:** use a light label chip, and remove the duplicate "retina" label so it does not crossfade with another one.
- 24.0-25.5 s: the grey blurred mouse holds still for about 3 s. **Fix:** split the screen into "you: sharp, cat: blurry" with the same mouse, or animate the blur sliding on.

**P2**
- 12-15 s: the mascot's "?" mark sits on the green mirror strip. Move the strip right, or keep the pop-mark zone clear.
- 7.5 s: the caption fades in after its words have started. Show captions at full opacity by the first spoken syllable.
- Ending: some cats glow blue, as Siamese cats do. "Green or gold?" is fine, but expect "blue" in the comments, and reply to them.

## ep 56: Pen cap hole (28.1 s), 6/10

**P1**

The two fingers float beside the cap without touching it, like sausages. **Fix:** show the cap at about 30° with a clearly dark round hole and a thin beam coming through it, and draw a hand that actually pinches the cap. A real phone clip of Trường holding a cap to a window would be ideal.
- 3.5-5.5 s: the pen with the "to help the ink flow?" wrong guess is a thin horizontal line in the top third of the frame, with the rest empty. **Fix:** a large diagonal pen filling the frame, with an ink-drop icon next to the guess.

- 10.0-12.0 s: the arrow through the cap points up at 10 s and 12 s and down at 11 s. Without a label this looks inconsistent. **Fix:** label "breathe in / out", or show a single flow direction.

**P2**

- 13.5 s: the forceps ("doctor gets it out") are two faint white lines. Make them visible or cut them.

## ep 57: Wooden spoon (32.1 s), 5/10

**P0**
- **3.5-8.0 s, a 4.5 s near-still stretch right before the 10 s wall.** After the spoon lands (3.0 s) and the foam drops (3.5 s), the frame is a flat pot. Only text changes ("pressing it down?", then crossed out). The push-in starts only at 8.0 s. **Fix:** move the push-in to the spoon contact to about 4.5 s, and show bubbles visibly rising and popping on the wood with pop SFX every 0.5 s. Cut the "pressing it down?" scene to 1.5 s, or overlay it on the push-in.

**P1**
- 0.0 s: the hook text says "about to boil over", but frame 0 already shows foam running down the sides. **Fix:** start with the foam at the rim and rising, then let it spill over at about 1 s.
- 10.0-12.0 s: the bubbles bursting are drawn as thin dashed rings. A pop has no flash or burst. **Fix:** a bright burst plus droplets, with a 3-frame white flash on each pop.
- 13.5-15.0 s: the "starch bubble" is a dark brown sphere and reads as a meatball or planet. **Fix:** a translucent bubble with a rim highlight, and a yellow sticky coating shown forming, like a skin wrapping around the bubble.
- 22.5 s: the chain reaction empties the whole foam tower. That overclaims: the spoon only keeps the foam below itself. **Fix:** stop the collapse at the spoon line.
- Ending, 29-32 s: "Have you tried it?" is a weak yes/no question with nothing to argue about. **Fix:** "Wood or metal spoon?" or "Does yours boil over?".
- 32 s is the longest of the six, and the fail payoff (foam climbing over at 28.5 s) collides with the question. Trim 2-3 s from the starch section.

**P2**

- 4.0 s and 25.5 s: the captions are almost invisible at the start of their words ("and the foam backs off", "it stops working").

## ep 58: Stopped clock (29.2 s), 6/10

**P0**
- **8.0-21.0 s, 13 s of abstract diagrams in a row.** First an eye plus a "your view" box that flickers between OFF, dot and clock (8-11.5 s). Then a strip of film-like frames with a dot, a dot and clocks (12-15 s). Then a horizontal bar chart of "tick 1 / borrowed / tick 2 / tick 3" (16.5-21 s). All of it is small, set on purple, and labelled at about 24 px. This is the worst retention risk of the six. **Fix:** keep the same big clock on screen. Show the second hand frozen with a "borrowed" glow while a quick "eye jump" whoosh crosses the screen. Then show a stopwatch overlay "felt: 1.5 s / real: 1.0 s". Use one metaphor, one object, and change something every 2 s.

**P1**
- **0-4.5 s, the test is staged weakly.** The pink dot (about 40 px) sits right next to the clock's rim. The eye jump is short, and the clock is the brightest thing, so most viewers are already looking at it. At about 4.4 s the clock moves and shrinks and new text appears, which disturbs the 2-3 ticks that viewers need to compare. Measured: the hand ticks exactly once per second from 0 s, which is correct. **Fix:** put a large dot (100 px or more) at the bottom-left, with the clock top-right, so the jump covers more than half the screen. Show "look at the dot" for 1.5 s, then "JUMP" with a whoosh, then keep at least 3 clean ticks with nothing else moving.
- 0.0 s thumbnail: a plain clock on purple, with nothing odd in it. **Fix:** at frame 0, show the second hand mid-"freeze" (a motion-trail ghost) or a giant pink dot with "Stare here" pointing at it.
- 1.0-7.0 s: the mascot's "?" mark sits on the clock face at 9 o'clock, inside the area the viewer is meant to watch. Move it.
- 22.5-24 s: the window, book, phone and mug icons with jump lines are tiny and read as a random diagram. **Fix:** show 3 eye jumps on the clock itself with a counter.

**P2**
- 1.0 s: the caption "Now jump your eyes" is faded during its own words.
- "real: 1 s" and "felt: up to 1.5 s" are at about 22 px.

**P1**

**P2**

## Cross-video patterns (what the next episodes must do differently)

1. **The 7-15 s window is still where the diagrams go, and the diagrams are abstract.** Five of six videos switch at about 7-8 s from a recognisable object to a schematic on a dark background: an eye ring, bars, stripes, a timeline, a grid. That is exactly where viewers leave (9-10 s). Rule: **the hook object stays on screen through 15 s.** Explain by pushing in on it or cutting it open in place, never by cutting to a new abstract diagram. Any schematic must contain the hook object or its outline, fill at least 60 % of the frame and be bright.
2. **Frame 0 still lacks the "weird result".** Only ep 55 shows the weird thing in frame 0. Ep 60 shows a calm face, ep 59 a cat that looks dead, ep 58 a plain clock, ep 56 a glare instead of the hole, ep 57 foam already over. Rule: **frame 0 shows the strange state at its peak** (the pain, the glow, the hole with light coming through it, the foam about to spill). Check it at 120 px wide: can a stranger name the object and see what is odd?
3. **Small things on screen.** Labels at 22-28 px, hairline arrows and rays, 90 px photos, 40 px dots. On a phone these vanish. Rule: **nothing important under 40 px of text or 8 px of line width.** One label at a time, at least 44 px.
4. **Payoff timing and still stretches.** Ep 57 pays off at 3.5 s and then holds still. Ep 56 and ep 55 have 2.5-4 s still holds. Rule: **every 2 s something moves on the main object** (not just text), checked across all of 0-15 s, with a scheduled event at 8-9 s.
5. **The template is identical in all six:** a small static mascot at the bottom-left, the same title box and caption box, dark gradients (three of six are the same maroon), no real footage. This does not meet v5 rules 3 and 4 (a real clip, and a large mascot that reacts). Pop marks (?, !, a bulb) keep landing on the subject. Rule: **use at least one real phone clip, or a recognisable photo-like object, per Short. Let the mascot react big at the reveal** (scale it up and move it) or remove it from the explanation scenes.

# Why It Does That — channel setup

Everything below goes into YouTube Studio. Text is ready to paste.

## 1. Customization → Profile

**Name:** Why It Does That

**Handle:** @WhyItDoesThat-hell (current). A handle without the suffix reads cleaner to an English-speaking audience if one is free, e.g. @WhyItDoesThat or @WhyItDoesThatShow.

**Picture:** `channel-avatar.png` (800×800)

**Banner:** `channel-banner.png` (2560×1440; the title sits inside the area visible on every device)

**Description:**

Why is a rainbow always at the same angle? Why do traffic jams appear out of nowhere? Why do wheels spin backwards on camera?

Why It Does That takes one everyday thing you have seen a thousand times and shows the mechanism behind it. Not as a drawing, but as a simulation running the real physics on screen.

One question per video. One mechanism. No formula without a picture.

Long videos go deep on a single "why". Shorts give you one surprising fact and the reason behind it.

How these videos are made: the scripts, narration and animation are produced with AI tools, and the narration is a synthetic voice. Every explanation is checked against published sources, which are listed in each video's description. Spotted a mistake? Say so in the comments and a correction gets pinned.

**Description (English):**

Why do we yawn when someone else does? Why do traffic jams appear out of nowhere? How do headphones cancel noise with more noise?

Why It Does That answers the everyday "why" questions you never got round to asking: your body, your brain, the tech in your pocket, the physics around you and the weird rules of nature. Each video takes one question and shows the mechanism behind it as an animated simulation, so you can see the answer, not just hear it.

New videos every week: long explainers and Shorts.

How these videos are made: scripts, narration and animation are produced with AI tools, and the voice is synthetic. Every claim is checked against published sources, listed in each video's description. Spotted a mistake? Tell us in the comments and a correction gets pinned.

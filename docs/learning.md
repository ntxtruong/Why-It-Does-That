## Self-learning loop (Trường, 2026-11-07)

Every run improves the next one. Steps:
1. **Measure (Stage 0, needs Studio):** for every Short Public for 48 h or more, record in the table below: views, Viewed vs Swiped Away %, average % viewed, average view duration, subscribers gained. Studio → Analytics → Content → Shorts, or the video's own analytics page.
2. **Compare:** put the numbers next to what changed (v1 vs v2 style, length, topic pillar, hook type). Say plainly when the sample is too small to conclude anything (fewer than about 5 Shorts per group, or under 200 views each).
3. **Self-review before render:** contact sheet of every scene, scored against the rubric below; fix anything under target before the full render. After render, `qc_video.py` lit area ≥ 25 % per scene (except a deliberate dark beat) and no frozen stretch.
4. **Write one lesson** into Learnings per run, with the evidence (a number or a frame), and change the rubric only when the evidence supports it.

Rubric (Shorts v2): payoff image readable at thumbnail size in frame one · question on screen at 0 s · burned-in phrase captions, key words gold · the answer before 20 s · a visible change at least every 3–5 s · subject fills 380–1250 px · nothing important below 1536 px or right of 940 px · no fade to an empty frame · last frame loops into the first · 35–45 s.

## Learnings

- 2026-11-25 (eps 51–52, no browser again, two Opus build subagents): (1) L1 could not score anything: eps 45–52 are all waiting for upload, so the loop has no feedback on v4 yet; the bottleneck is now a browser run, not making more Shorts. (2) A build subagent may come without the Agent tool: one builder (ep 51) ran its own "independent" fact-check and cold read, the other used `claude -p`. Rule: the coordinator always runs the web-search fact-check itself after a builder reports unless the builder shows it used a separate agent; on ep 51 it found no error, only nuance (the hum fades over a moment, no duration is published). (3) Both builders met the layout rules on the first contact sheet when the brief listed them up front (lit area 29–40 %), confirming the 11-20 lesson.
- 2026-11-20 (later, eps 47–50, Trường in chat): two build subagents (Opus, one topic each: Short + long) ran in parallel with two stops each (scripts → independent Sonnet fact-check; contact sheets → independent Sonnet cold read), Haiku for translations. ~2.5 h wall, renders slowed to ~40 min per long video with both rendering. What worked: the fact-check again hedged real overreaches ("many lights" → "some lights", 120 Hz at 29.97 fps → "almost still", laptop PWM → "some laptop screens", freezer ice is already below 0 °C). What cost time: (1) a subagent's SendMessage to the coordinator only queues while the coordinator waits on it; a subagent must hand back (end its turn) at each stop; (2) the cold read sent back a full re-layout of both flicker videos (small charts, text under 40 px, empty half-frames, calm frame one); brief builders to meet the layout rules (fill the frame, text ≥ 40 px, the hook visible in frame one) BEFORE the first contact sheet; (3) one builder stopped on an Opus 5-hour rate limit and had to be resumed after the reset.

- 2026-11-20 (eps 45–46, first v4 Shorts, no browser): (1) No new numbers, so nothing in L1 could be scored; H1–H10 stand as on 11-18. (2) `qc_video.py` lit area misreads light backgrounds: the blue-banana stimulus on light grey, the brightest frame we have made, scored 0.9 % (it counts pixels brighter than the frame's median). Judge lit area only on dark-background scenes. (3) The fact-check changed a design, not just words: a colour afterimage needs ~30–60 s for a strong effect, so a 9 s stare was lengthened to 11 s, the narration never promises the result, and the description says "faint" and "stare longer". Rule: for any try-it Short, check that the effect works at the length the Short allows before building. (4) A new `tts.py` option `"gaps"` (per-beat pause, seconds) times a still stretch with the voice; `build.js` reads `window.NOPUSH` / `window.NODIP` (scene ids with no push-in or dip) for scenes that must hold still on the retina. (5) The cold read (Sonnet, contact sheets) again caught real problems: banner and voice gave two different instructions ("Don't blink" vs "Do not move your eyes"), the banana read as a smile until tilted, "breaks again" sat on a hand, labels on the wave appeared ~3 s before the voice said them (slowed the wave so the second break lands on "breaks again").

- 2026-11-18, **why views are low** (Trường: "mọi người xem rất ít"; table in `research/shorts-results.md`): avg view is 10–14 s for the best and 4–6 s for the worst whatever the length (21–55 s); eps 31–40 (21–27 s, claim-first openers) got 37–160 views each, ep 43 (do-it-yourself) 890. Causes in order of evidence: (1) flat claim or abstract-question openers, (2) explain-first scripts with nothing new at 7–10 s, (3) too many thin Shorts (10 in two days) so none got time to be good, (4) dark diagram look with small subjects (lit area 17–24 %, unverified as a cause). Not yet known: whether the voice is a factor (Claude cannot hear it), and the retention curve of each Short (only the averages were read). Action: Shorts v4 playbook and `research/`. Mistake of mine: I called it a hook problem from averages only; the retention graph of the best and worst Short has to be read next run to confirm where people leave.

- 2026-11-17 (eps 43–44): the cold read caught that frame one showed the same remote twice with no reason ("why is there a remote by 'your eyes'?"); relabelled "to your eyes" / "on camera". Lit area vs payoff: lightening the phone screens to lift `qc_video.py` lit area washed out the infrared glow that *is* the payoff; a mid-tone screen plus a stronger violet halo kept the glow readable at thumbnail size (lit 22–33 %). Avoid initials in narration or fix the splitter. The ASR check still hears "isn't" as "is in" (ep 41, twice): write "is not" in narration from now on. A long video with ~490 words at speed 0.9 and 1.5 s pauses on key beats comes out ~3:55; aim for ~560 words to reach the 4–5 min target. Long renders run ~8–12 fps here (7,000 frames ≈ 12 min).

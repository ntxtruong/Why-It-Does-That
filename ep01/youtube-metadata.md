## Description

A rainbow looks like it's painted on the sky, but it moves when you move and you can never get closer to it. That's because a rainbow isn't an object. It's an angle: 42 degrees.

In this video we send light through a single raindrop, one ray at a time, and watch where it comes out. Every ray in this video is traced with the real law of refraction, not drawn by hand.

How this video was made
Script, narration and animation were produced with AI tools. The narration uses a synthetic voice. The animations are computer simulations of light refracting and reflecting in a spherical water drop (refractive index 1.330 for red to 1.343 for violet).

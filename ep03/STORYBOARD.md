# Episode 3 — STORYBOARD (rebuilt)

**Logline:** A camera samples in frames; when a spoke moves more than half a gap between frames, the brain matches it to the wrong spoke, and the wheel runs backwards.

**World:** Brand kit v2. A film-strip world: the screen is a light table; frames are cards that slide in from the right. The real wheel lives in a "continuous" lane at the top (gold marked spoke visible), the camera's view in a "frames" lane below (no mark). Real motion `blue`, perceived motion `coral` arrows, the marked spoke `gold`.
**Colour arc:** neutral → coral confusion (reversal) → gold clarity (the rule) → coral again under flicker light → resolved.
**Recurring motif:** the *two lanes* — what happened vs what the camera saw — and the *spoke clock*: a dial showing per-frame rotation against half a gap, with a forward/backward boundary.
**Mascot:** holds a tiny clapperboard; eyes follow the wrong spoke, then squints when the gold mark reveals the truth.
**Different from ep1, 2, 4, 5:** light-table + film-strip lanes, sampling dial (not a map, not a cross-section, not an oscilloscope).

**Simulation:** wheel angle θ(t) closed form; frame k shows θ(k/fps); apparent motion = wrap(Δθ, ±gap/2). Shutter blur = average of 8 sub-samples within the exposure. Flicker = brightness pulses at 100 Hz rendered as sampled frames.

# Episode 4 — STORYBOARD

**Logline:** A jam with no cause is a wave made of late, slightly-too-hard reactions; on a crowded road it is inevitable, and one calm driver can dissolve it.

**World:** Brand kit v2 (background `#18264F`, panel `#23356A`). Seen from straight above, like a city at night drawn by a draughtsman: lanes as thin `quiet` lines, cars as rounded capsules with a soft headlight cone. Speed is colour: moving cars `blue`, braking cars shading to `coral`, the computer car `gold`.
**Colour arc:** cool and even (free flow) → coral knot spreading (jam) → a single gold car whose calm spreads blue again → ending back on the free-flowing ring.
**Recurring motif:** the *ring* seen from above, which becomes a *space-time strip* (time down the screen, position across): every car draws a line and a jam shows as a diagonal coral band sloping backwards. The viewer learns to read it once and then sees it in every chapter.
**Mascot:** in a tiny car of its own in the chain shot; reacts (squint, brake-lights flare) when the slowdown reaches it.
**Visual language different from ep1–3:** top-down map + space-time diagram (ep1: raindrop optics close-up; ep2: atmosphere cross-section; ep3: spinning wheel + film strip).

**Simulation:** optimal-velocity model (Bando 1995) on a 230 m ring with 22 cars, integrated once at load time with a fixed step and a fixed seeded initial perturbation; frames read from the precomputed array (pure function of t). Parameters tuned so the jam forms in the "ring" chapter and moves backwards at a speed that matches ~20 km/h at the shown scale. Arizona chapter: same model with one car switched to a "follow the average speed" controller.

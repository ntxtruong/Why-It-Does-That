# Episode 5 — STORYBOARD

**Logline:** Noise-cancelling headphones are fast mirror makers: they play the inverse of the noise so the two cancel, which works brilliantly for low, steady hums and fails for high, changing voices, because of timing and the size of the quiet zone.

**World:** Brand kit v2. A clean side-on cross-section world: air drawn as columns of fine dots (≈2,500) whose spacing shows pressure (squeeze = dots crowd, stretch = dots spread). Noise is `coral`, anti-noise `blue`, their sum `gold` (a flat gold line = silence).
**Colour arc:** coral-dominated loud opening → blue mirror appears → gold flat line (quiet) → coral leaking back at high pitch → balanced end with the gold line.
**Recurring motif:** the *three stacked traces* (coral noise, blue anti-noise, gold sum) under every scene, like an oscilloscope. The viewer learns: flat gold = quiet.
**Mascot:** wears oversized headphones; its ear-tufts stop fluttering when the gold line goes flat.
**Different from ep1–4:** cross-section of air with pressure dots + oscilloscope traces + 2-D interference map (ep4 is a top-down map + space-time strip).

**Simulation:** closed-form plane and spherical waves, p(x,t) = A·sin(kx − ωt + φ); dot positions are analytic displacements (no integration). Interference map: sum of two point sources evaluated per pixel on a low-res grid, upscaled. Zone-of-quiet bubble: contour where |p_sum|/|p_noise| ≤ 1/√10, size tied to λ/10.

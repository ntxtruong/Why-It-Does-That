# Episode 7 — STORYBOARD

**Logline:** Fingers wrinkle in water not only because skin soaks, but because nerves tell fingertip blood vessels to tighten; how it works is clear, why we have it is still argued over.

**World:** Brand kit v2. Two scales that the camera travels between: (a) a stylised hand in a bath, seen from above, water as `blue` caustics; (b) a giant cut-away of a fingertip: stratum corneum as a sheet of tiled cells (cream on panel `#23356A`), dermis with anchor points (gold pins), a branching vessel tree (coral blood), a nerve fibre drawn as a thin gold line with travelling pulses.
**Colour arc:** warm coral-rich fingertip (full of blood) → coral fades as vessels narrow → cool, folded, blue-washed skin → coral returns at "dry off".
**Recurring motif:** the *surface line*: the top edge of the skin is always one continuous gold-edged line. Smooth line = no wrinkles. Every chapter ends on the line, flat or folded.
**Character:** chibi3 (Trường-style chibi) in a small bath-tub inset at the left, lifting his hand to look at his fingers at each chapter end; a little "?" over him in the why chapter.
**Different from ep 1–6:** biology cut-away with a nerve-signal chain and a mechanics buckling simulation; two-scale camera zoom (ep 6 is a single macro cross-section).

**Simulation (deterministic):** buckling of a thin stiff layer on a soft foundation, closed form: wrinkle wavelength λ = 2πh·(E_s/3E_f)^{1/3}, amplitude A = h·√(ε/ε_c − 1) for strain ε above critical ε_c; strain ε(t) = swell(t) + shrink(t) as smooth easing curves, so the viewer sees (1) swell alone → ripples barely start, (2) shrink alone → barely, (3) both → clear folds (qualitative match to Sáez & Zöllner 2017; no numbers on screen). Anchors pin the line at fixed x positions; between them the profile is A·sin² shape. Vessel radius r(t) eased; blood dots flow along precomputed paths, density ∝ r². Nerve pulses are phase = (t·v − s) mod spacing.

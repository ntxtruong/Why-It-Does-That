**Misconception corrected:** "fingers wrinkle because skin soaks up water". Swelling contributes, but without the nerve signal there is no wrinkling.

**Hedged on screen and in narration:** the electrolyte trigger (#7), the 3-person repeatability study (#11), the ancestors/grip line (framed as a hypothesis).

**Added after skeptic review (2026-11-02):** #11 — the interpretation that the underlying blood vessels set the repeatable pattern is the authors' own (German et al. 2025, as reported by ScienceAlert and Live Science); narration keeps "suggests".

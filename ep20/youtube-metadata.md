**Title** (43 chars): Why Do Onions Make You Cry? #onion #science

**Description**

```
A whole onion doesn't make you cry; a cut one does. Each cell keeps a harmless sulfur compound and an enzyme apart. Your knife mixes them, and a second enzyme turns the result into a stinging gas that reaches the nerves on the surface of your eye. Tears wash it away. Chilling the onion slows the enzymes.
```

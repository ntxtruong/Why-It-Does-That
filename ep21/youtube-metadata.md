**Title** (44 chars): Why Does a Cold Can Sweat? #science #physics

**Description**

```
The water on a cold can comes from the air, not from inside. On a hot, humid day (32 °C, 75% humidity) a cubic metre of air carries about 25 g of water vapour. The air touching the can cools to nearly fridge temperature, and at about 4 °C no more than about 6 g per cubic metre can stay as vapour. The rest condenses on the can, like morning dew.
```

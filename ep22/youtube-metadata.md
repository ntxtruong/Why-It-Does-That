**Title** (56 chars): Why Does a Spoon Look Broken in Water? #science #physics

**Description**

```
A spoon in water isn't bent: the light is. Light travels about a quarter slower in water than in air (refractive index 1.33), so a ray leaving the water at a slant bends at the surface. Your brain traces each ray straight back, so the underwater part of the spoon appears lifted, and the spoon seems to snap right at the surface.
```

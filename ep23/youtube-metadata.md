**Title** (59 chars): Why Does a Spinning Coin Speed Up Before It Stops? #physics

**Description**

```
A spinning coin is not speeding up: it is losing energy. What speeds up is the point where its edge touches the table. As the coin leans lower, that touching point races around faster (for an ideal rolling disc the rate grows like one over the square root of the tilt), so the rattle rises to tens of laps a second before the coin drops flat. Shown slowed down 12 times; laps per second computed for a 12 mm coin.
```

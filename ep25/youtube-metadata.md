**Title** (55 chars): Why Does Toast Land Butter-Side Down? #science #physics

**Description**

```
It's not bad luck and it's not the butter: it's mostly the height of your table. Toast sliding off the edge pivots on the rim and starts to spin. From a 75 cm table the fall takes about 0.4 seconds, only enough for about half a turn, so butter-up becomes butter-down. From a table about 3 m high it would turn roughly a full turn and land butter up.

Caveat: a fast sideways flick changes the outcome; this is for toast that slides or is nudged off.
```

**Title** (56 chars): Why Does Your Phone Go Dark Through Sunglasses? #science

**Description**

```
Your screen never switched off: your polarized sunglasses blocked it. Many phone screens send out light that wiggles one way only (polarized light). Polarized sunglasses are a filter too: worn upright they pass up-and-down wiggles, to block glare off roads and water. Lined up, the light gets through; turned a quarter turn, the filters cross and almost nothing passes (Malus's law, I = I₀ cos²θ). Tilt your head and the picture usually comes back.

Caveat: many phones set their polarization at 45° or add a film so the screen stays visible either way; then it only dims. Which orientation goes dark depends on the model.
```

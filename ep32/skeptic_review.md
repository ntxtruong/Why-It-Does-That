# Skeptic review (Sonnet subagent, 2026-11-14) and what changed
- "A predicted touch feels weaker" was circular → "So it turns that touch down, and the tickle fades."
- "touched their own palm through a robot" was vague → "people moved one hand, and a robot stroked their other palm" (Blakemore et al. 1999 setup).
- "only mutes" overreached (attenuation, not zero) → "turns down what it sees coming".
- "a fifth of a second … tickled more" kept: 200 ms delay, progressive increase in rated tickliness from 0 to 200 ms; not claimed equal to someone else's touch.

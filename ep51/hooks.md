# Ep 51 hook lines (hook type DO)

On-screen text in frame one: "Pinch your nose. Now hum." (5 words), over a hand already closing on the nose of a large side-view chibi head.

1. **"Close your lips, pinch your nose, and hum."** (DO) **← picked.** The first spoken line is the instruction itself; the viewer can do it in 2 s and hears the result in their own head, so the payoff is guaranteed and seen (waveform) as well as felt. No claim is spoken first.
2. "Your hum has one second to live." (COUNTDOWN) — rejected: puts a number on a duration the sources do not give ("a short time"), and promising a time risks overselling.
3. "Can you hum with your nose shut?" (GUESS / question) — rejected: question openers did worst in our numbers (swipe file, CLAUDE.md 2026-11-18), and most people already guess "yes" without trying, so there is no action in the first 2 s.

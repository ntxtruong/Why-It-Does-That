## Fact-check (independent Sonnet subagent with web search, 2026-11-25)
Result: no factual error; crossed-disparity direction in the drawing confirmed (Webvision, NCBI Bookshelf NBK11545); "looking back makes it vanish" and "pulling apart shrinks it / it floats" confirmed (Bach, Myers). Changes applied:
- Line 1 hedged: "a hand's length" → "about a hand's length" (sources range 13–30 cm; do not state one distance as a rule).
- Line 7: "you get the floating finger" → "you see a floating finger".
- Line 6 kept as a simplification ("lays both views on top of each other"); the checker noted Bach adds rivalry/suppression. The drawing shows the non-overlapping parts faint, which is the suppression in picture form; not voiced.
- Never promise "instantly": the description says most people see it, it can look blurred at first, and it may not work for people whose two eyes do not work together well (Bach: needs "two roughly equivalent eyes").
- Shortened for length (not from the check): line 4 "Pull them apart a bit. It floats.", line 5 "Your eyes aim far, so…".

# Ep 52 hook lines (hook type DO / WEIRD)

Frame one in every case: two big light fingertips moving toward each other over a blurred far room. Spoken first line is the instruction ("Touch your fingertips, a hand's length from your eyes.").

1. **"Make a floating finger"** (DO + WEIRD, 4 words) **← picked.** It is a task the viewer can do in 5 s, it names a strange result without explaining it, and the video pays it off on screen at about 7–8 s (a solid floating finger between two ghostly ones). Readable at thumbnail size in two lines.
2. "Grow a third finger" (WEIRD, 4 words). Stronger as a tease, but oversells: what appears is a short sausage with two tips, not a finger with a nail, and v4 rule 7 says an unpaid tease costs more than a plain opener.
3. "Touch fingertips. Look past them." (DO, 5 words). Honest and clear, but it is only an instruction: no promise of a result, so nothing tells a scroller why to try it; our data says the strange result on screen is what keeps people.

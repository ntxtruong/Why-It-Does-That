# Ep 56 claims (pen cap hole)
| Claim | Source |
| --- | --- |
| ISO 11540 covers caps of writing/marking instruments likely to be used by children up to 14 | ISO 11540:2021 abstract (standards.iteh.ai catalogue page) |
| Ventilated cap: at least 8 L/min of air at a pressure drop of at most 1.33 kPa | ISO 11540:2021, clause 4.3 (iteh catalogue summary); Mental Floss |
| Alternative: cap too big to inhale (does not pass a 16 mm ring gauge with 5 mm left outside) | ISO 11540:2021, clause 4.2 (iteh summary) |
| The hole is a safety feature against choking, not for the ink | Mental Floss ("nothing to do with how the pen works"); Reader's Digest; BIC statements quoted in press |
| Caps are sometimes inhaled by children (3–8 % of foreign-body aspiration in a 2012 study; 34 of 1,280 cases in a Beijing series 1997–2007) | Mental Floss, citing the studies |
| A lodged cap is removed by a doctor (bronchoscopy) | Mental Floss / case reports (general medical practice) |
Notes: "kids" in narration = children up to 14 (the standard's scope). Air flow is shown as eight 1-litre balloons, schematic. The windpipe drawing is schematic, not to scale. Narration avoids "ISO" (TTS risk); the number is on screen only.

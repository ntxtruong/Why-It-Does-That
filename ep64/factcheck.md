# Ep 64 fact-check (ruler drop test)
Checked 2026-11-26.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Note / source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ruler drop measures reaction time, t = sqrt(2d/g) | VERIFIED | https://topendsports.com/testing/tests/reaction-stick.htm (formula t = sqrt(2d/g)); https://www.npl.co.uk/skills-learning/measurement-at-home/reaction-time |
| 2 | "Most people catch it here, at twenty" | FIX | Sources: SciAm says partners "usually catch the ruler at about 15 cm" (~0.17 s); Topend Sports table puts "average" at 24-44 cm (0.22-0.30 s), "good" at 16-24 cm. | 3 | "Not slow fingers": delay is mostly seeing/processing | VERIFIED (softened) | SciAm: most of the delay is brain processing and nerve signalling; the article does not time muscle vs brain separately. Say "mostly" in the description. |
| 4 | Ruler is a clock; 5 cm=0.1 s, 20 cm=0.2 s, 30 cm~0.25 s | VERIFIED | Topend table: 5 cm 0.101 s, 20 cm 0.202 s, 30 cm 0.247 s. |
| 5 | 20 cm is a fifth of a second | VERIFIED | 0.202 s at 20 cm (Topend); 19.6 cm exact. |
| 6 | Eye -> brain -> fingers | VERIFIED (schematic) | SciAm: eye to visual cortex, motor cortex, spinal cord, arm/hand muscles. |
| 7 | Falls faster and faster | VERIFIED | Free fall, constant g, v = g t (~2 m/s at 0.2 s). |
| 8 | 0.3 s -> ~44 cm, past a 30 cm ruler | VERIFIED | 0.5*9.81*0.09 = 44.1 cm; Topend gives 40 cm = 0.286 s. Drawn 30 cm ruler assumption: 0 end at the fingers. |

Narration lines: "Pinch your fingers the instant it drops." fine. "Most people catch it here, at twenty. It's not slow fingers." -> change "Most people" to "Many people" / "around twenty". "This ruler is a clock." fine. "Twenty centimetres is a fifth of a second: eye, to brain, to fingers." accurate. "It falls faster and faster. Wait three tenths, and it's gone." accurate for a 30 cm ruler.

Description note: typical range 0.15-0.30 s (SciAm); dropper should wait a random time (Topend: release "without warning").
